# 📊 SaaS E-commerce Cohort Retention & CLTV Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **SaaS E-commerce Cohort Retention & CLTV Analysis** project focuses on analyzing customer behavior through cohort analysis, retention metrics, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV). Using SQL, Python, and Power BI, this project transforms raw customer and transaction data into actionable business insights to improve customer retention, reduce churn, and maximize long-term revenue.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer acquisition cohorts.
- Measure customer retention over time.
- Calculate Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
- Identify customer churn patterns.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Generate data-driven business insights and recommendations.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Customer retention declines significantly after the first few months.
- Returning customers generate higher lifetime value than one-time customers.
- Certain acquisition cohorts perform better in long-term retention.
- Improving customer onboarding can significantly increase retention and CLTV.
- High-value customers contribute a substantial share of total revenue.
