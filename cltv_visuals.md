# CLTV Charts

## Objective
Create visualizations to analyze Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) and its key drivers.

## Visuals

### 1. CLTV by Customer Segment
- Visual: Clustered Bar Chart
- Y-axis: Customer Segment
- X-axis: CLTV

### 2. Average Order Value (AOV) by Customer Segment
- Visual: Clustered Column Chart
- X-axis: Customer Segment
- Y-axis: Average Order Value (AOV)

### 3. Purchase Frequency by Customer Segment
- Visual: Clustered Column Chart
- X-axis: Customer Segment
- Y-axis: Purchase Frequency
