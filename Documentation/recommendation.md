Business Recommendations (Based on CLTV Analysis)
🎯 1. Focus on High-Value Customers (High CLTV)

👉 These customers generate the maximum revenue

✅ Actions:
Offer loyalty programs
Provide exclusive discounts / early access
Personalized recommendations

💡 Goal: Retain them longer → increase profit

🎯 2. Improve Retention for Low CLTV Customers

👉 Many customers have:

Low lifespan
Few purchases
✅ Actions:
Send re-engagement emails
Offer first-time discounts
Improve onboarding experience

💡 Goal: Convert low-value → repeat customers

🎯 3. Convert Medium CLTV → High CLTV

👉 These customers have growth potential

✅ Actions:
Upselling (premium products)
Cross-selling (related items)
Bundle offers

💡 Goal: Increase purchase frequency

🎯 4. Optimize Marketing Spend

👉 Use CLTV to guide spending

✅ Actions:
Spend more on high CLTV segments
Reduce spend on low-value users
Target lookalike audiences

💡 Goal: Improve ROI

🎯 5. Reduce Customer Churn

👉 Many users drop early

✅ Actions:
Identify churn patterns
Improve product/service quality
Provide better support

💡 Goal: Increase customer lifespan

🎯 6. Pricing & Product Strategy

👉 Based on purchase behavior

✅ Actions:
Introduce bundle pricing
Offer subscription models
Promote high-margin products

💡 Goal: Increase average order value

🎯 7. Data-Driven Decision Making

👉 Use analytics continuously

✅ Actions:
Track CLTV over time
Monitor segment performance
Update strategies regularly

💡 Goal: Continuous business improvement
