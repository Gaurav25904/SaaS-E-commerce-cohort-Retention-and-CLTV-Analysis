Customer Retention Insights 
🔹 1. Overall Retention Behavior
The retention matrix shows a declining trend over time.
Most customers make a purchase only once and do not return.
Retention drops significantly after the first month.

👉 Insight:
This indicates a low customer loyalty rate and high churn.

🔹 2. First Month Retention (Baseline = 100%)
All cohorts start with 100% retention in month 0.
This confirms that the cohort analysis is correctly built.

👉 Insight:
The dataset is valid and structured properly for retention analysis.

🔹 3. Sharp Drop After First Purchase
Retention falls drastically from Month 0 → Month 1.
Only a small percentage of users return in the next month.

👉 Insight:

Customers are not engaged after first purchase
Possible issues:
Poor customer experience
No follow-up marketing
Low product stickiness
🔹 4. Long-Term Retention is Very Low
After 3–6 months, retention becomes very small (close to 0).
Very few customers stay active long-term.

👉 Insight:

Business lacks long-term retention strategy
Needs:
Loyalty programs
Discounts / offers
Email remarketing
🔹 5. Cohort Comparison
Some cohorts perform slightly better than others.
This may depend on:
Marketing campaigns
Seasonal demand
Promotions

👉 Insight:

Not all acquisition strategies are equal
Identify high-performing cohorts and replicate them
🔹 6. Revenue vs Retention Connection
Low retention directly affects monthly revenue stability
Business depends heavily on new customers instead of repeat users

👉 Insight:

Growth is acquisition-driven, not retention-driven
Increasing retention will improve profitability
🔹 7. Business Problem Identified
High customer churn
Low repeat purchase rate

👉 Insight:
This is a classic case where:
➡️ “Getting customers is easy, keeping them is hard”

💡 Recommendations 
✅ Improve Retention Strategy
Email marketing campaigns
Personalized offers
Push notifications
✅ Loyalty Programs
Reward repeat customers
Points / cashback system
✅ Customer Engagement
Follow-ups after purchase
Feedback collection
✅ Target High-Performing Cohorts
Analyze best cohorts
Reuse successful campaigns
