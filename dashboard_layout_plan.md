# Power BI Dashboard Layout Plan

## Project
SaaS/E-Commerce Cohort Retention & CLTV Analysis

## Objective
Design an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze customer retention,
identify churn patterns, measure Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV), and
identify high-value and low-value customer segments.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

#### KPI Cards
- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Purchase Frequency
- Average CLTV
- Overall Retention Rate

#### Charts
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Monthly Customer Trend
- Revenue by Country
- Customer Segment Comparison

---

### 2. Cohort Retention Analysis

#### Main Visual
- Cohort Retention Heatmap
  - Rows: Cohort Month
  - Columns: Cohort Index (Month 0, Month 1, Month 2, etc.)
  - Values: Retention Rate (%)

#### Supporting Charts
- Retention Decay Curve
- Cohort Size Comparison
- Retention Rate by Cohort

---

### 3. CLTV Analysis

#### KPI Cards
- Average CLTV
- Average Order Value
- Purchase Frequency
- Average Customer Lifespan

#### Charts
- CLTV by Customer Segment
- AOV by Customer Segment
- Purchase Frequency by Segment
- CLTV Comparison

---

### 4. Customer Analysis

#### Customer Segments
- High-Value Customers
- Medium-Value Customers
- Low-Value Customers

#### Charts
- Customer Count by Segment
- Revenue Contribution by Segment
- CLTV by Segment
- Revenue by Country

---

## Dashboard Slicers

- Invoice Date
- Cohort Month
- Country
- Customer Segment


## Expected Outcome

The dashboard will help stakeholders identify customer churn patterns,
compare cohort retention performance, analyze customer lifetime value,
and identify high-value customer segments for retention and profitability
strategies.
