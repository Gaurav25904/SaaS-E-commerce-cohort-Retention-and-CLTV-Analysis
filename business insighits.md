BUSINESS INSIGHTS 


 1. Revenue Insights
 Insight:
The business generates consistent revenue with noticeable monthly variations.
Certain months show higher sales → indicates seasonal demand or campaign impact.
 Action:
Focus marketing during high-performing months
Investigate low-performing months


 2. Customer Behavior
 Insight:
A small group of customers contributes a large portion of revenue
Indicates high-value customers (Pareto principle 80/20 rule)
 Action:
Retain top customers with loyalty programs
Offer personalized discounts


3. Customer Retention
 Insight:
Repeat customers exist but retention rate is not very high
Indicates scope for improving customer loyalty
 Action:
Email marketing / remarketing
Reward repeat purchases


 4. Purchase Patterns
 Insight:
Customers make limited purchases → low frequency
Suggests one-time buyers dominate
 Action:
Bundle offers
Subscription models


 5. Product Performance
 Insight:
Few products generate majority revenue
Some products underperform
 Action:
Promote best-selling products
Remove or improve low-performing products


 6. Monthly Trends
 Insight:
Revenue trend shows growth/decline patterns over time
Useful for forecasting
 Action:
Plan inventory accordingly
Optimize ad spend timing

 
 7. Customer Segmentation (RFM)
 Insight:
Customers divided into:
Best customers 
Loyal customers 
Low-value customers
 Action:
Focus retention on high-value segments
Convert low-value → loyal


 8. Revenue Distribution
 Insight:
Revenue is unevenly distributed across customers
Indicates dependency on few customers
Action:
 Diversify customer base
 Increase average order value

Diversify customer base
Increase average order value
