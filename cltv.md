Basic CLTV Formula
1. Simple Formula:CLTV = Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan
2.Average Order Value (AOV):How much customer spends per order
   AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders
3.Purchase Frequency:How often customer buys
   Purchase Frequency = Total Orders / Total Customers
4.Customer Lifespan:How long they stay with business
  Customer Lifespan = Average months customer stays active
