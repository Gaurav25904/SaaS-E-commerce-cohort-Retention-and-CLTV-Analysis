 EDA Findings – Online Retail Dataset
1.  Monthly Revenue Trends
Revenue shows fluctuations across months, indicating possible seasonality
Certain months have significant spikes, likely due to:
Holiday seasons 
Promotional campaigns 
Some periods show decline, suggesting:
Low demand
Off-season behavior

 Business Insight:
Revenue is not consistent → company should focus on seasonal planning & promotions

2.  Monthly Active Customers
Number of unique customers varies month-to-month
Growth trend indicates:
Increasing adoption  OR
Marketing effectiveness

 Business Insight:
Tracking active customers helps measure business growth and engagement

3.  New vs Repeat Customers
Customers divided into:
New customers → first-time buyers
Repeat customers → returning users
Observations:
A healthy business has a balance of both
If repeat customers are high:
→ strong customer retention
If mostly new customers:
→ good acquisition but weak retention

Business Insight:
Retention is key — repeat customers drive long-term revenue

4.  Revenue Contribution Behavior
Repeat customers often contribute more revenue
High-frequency customers are valuable

 Business Insight:
Focus on loyal customers for higher profitability

5. Data Quality Observations
Missing CustomerID values found and removed
Data cleaning required before analysis

 Business Insight:
Raw data is messy — preprocessing is essential in real-world projects

 Final Summary
Business shows seasonal revenue patterns
Customer base is dynamic (changing monthly)
Retention vs acquisition balance is crucial
Data cleaning is necessary before insights
